SeraSupper

Meal planning for iOS

Plan the week. Skip the “what’s for dinner?”

SeraSupper helps you build a personal dish library, plan meals on a calendar, and turn them into a grocery list — with on-device AI for recipe ideas. Your data stays on your device and your private iCloud account.
