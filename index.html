Three things it does that others don’t

Dinner Dash: a week of dinners in about a minute

Standing in the store with no plan? Pick the meal and the week, and SeraSupper fills every night with something you’d actually eat, then builds the shopping list before you reach the checkout. Don’t like Thursday? One tap swaps it for the next-best idea.

Recipes written on your device

Need a recipe for a dish? SeraSupper writes one using Apple Intelligence, right on your phone — ingredients, steps and servings. Nothing leaves the device. Prefer a trusted site? Search the web inside the app and save the recipe to your library.

One grocery list from the whole plan

Every planned meal rolls up into a single list, duplicates merged and the staples you already have subtracted. Your pantry and your list sit side by side, so checking something off in the store keeps both honest.

Questions people ask before buying

Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 26.2 or later.
Do I need Apple Intelligence?
Only for AI recipe generation. Everything else — the dish library, calendar, Dinner Dash, web recipe saving, grocery lists, sync — works on any supported device. Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or a recent iPad, with it turned on in Settings.
Is it a subscription?
No. $4.99, once. No in-app purchases.
Where is my data?
On your device and in your own iCloud account. SeraSupper has no servers and no accounts, and the developer cannot see anything you enter. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is nothing to collect.
Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Yes, through iCloud, automatically.
Can I try it before it’s on the App Store?
Yes — the public beta is open on TestFlight. Feedback goes to support@harpertech.net.

About the app

SeraSupper is developed and published by Harper Advanced Technologies in Indianapolis. It’s in public beta ahead of its App Store release. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking — the app has no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy and the disclaimer on food safety and AI-generated content.
