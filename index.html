You’re at the store and you have no plan. Open Dinner Dash from the calendar menu, pick the meal you want to plan and the week, and you’ve got seven dinners and the shopping list to go with them. If you don’t like a meal the app has suggested, tap it and get another.

FAQ

Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 26.2 or later.
Do I need Apple Intelligence?
Only for a few extras: AI dish ideas, AI recipe drafts, and AI meal suggestions when adding a meal. Everything else works on any supported device: Dish Ideas from the built-in dish catalog, saving recipes from the web with their ingredients, the dish library, calendar, Dinner Dash, grocery lists, and sync. On an iPhone that can't run Apple Intelligence, the AI extras simply don't appear. Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or a recent iPad, with it turned on in Settings.
Is it a subscription?
No. $4.99, once. No in-app purchases.
Where is my data?
On your device and in your own iCloud account. SeraSupper has no servers and no accounts, and the developer cannot see anything you enter. The App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected” because there is nothing to collect.
Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Yes, through iCloud, automatically.
